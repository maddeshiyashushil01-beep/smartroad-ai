🚧 SmartRoad AI

AI-Powered Road Safety & Traffic Intelligence Platform

SmartRoad AI is a student-led project focused on using Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, GPS, and Web Technology to help identify road problems and understand traffic conditions.

🌍 The Problem

Cities face two connected challenges:

- 🕳️ Potholes and damaged roads can remain unnoticed for long periods.
- 🚗 Traffic congestion is difficult to monitor and understand across different locations.
- 📋 Citizen complaints and road-monitoring data are often separated.
- ⏱️ Manual inspection can take significant time.

💡 Our Idea

SmartRoad AI aims to bring these sources together into one platform.

🎯 Main Goals

- Detect potholes using computer vision.
- Estimate traffic density from road images/videos.
- Allow citizens to report road problems.
- Record the location of reported problems.
- Identify repeated reports of the same problem.
- Develop a road-priority system based on multiple factors.
- Present important information through a simple web dashboard.

❤️ Human Impact

The purpose of SmartRoad AI is not just to demonstrate AI.

The project explores how technology can help make road problems easier to report, easier to understand, and easier to prioritize.

The long-term goal is to investigate whether combining AI detection with citizen-generated information can provide useful insights for smarter road maintenance.

📊 Current Status

Status: 🚧 Early Development

SmartRoad AI is currently an educational and research prototype. Features will be added and tested incrementally.

🔬 Research Direction

The project will investigate questions such as:

- Can computer vision reliably detect road damage?
- How can traffic density influence road-maintenance priority?
- Can multiple citizen reports be intelligently grouped?
- How can road problems be ranked using multiple signals?
- What are the limitations of AI-based road monitoring?

⚠️ Disclaimer

This is currently a student research and development project. AI results are experimental and should not be treated as official road-condition assessments without appropriate verification.
